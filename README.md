# Nojun Kwak (곽노준)

**Professor**, Graduate School of Convergence Science and Technology (GSCST), Seoul National University  
**Director**, Machine Intelligence & Pattern Analysis (MIPAL) Laboratory  

---

## 👨‍🏫 Personal Statement

I am a Full Professor at Seoul National University leading the Machine Intelligence & Pattern Analysis (MIPAL) Laboratory. Since my graduate studies, I have been mainly working on the statistical analysis of input variables for pattern recognition problems. 

My primary research interests lie in advanced machine learning algorithms and their applications to computer vision. Specifically, I am deeply interested in detecting and tracking objects in real-world environments using computer vision algorithms, and their applications for autonomous vehicles and robotics. Recently, my research spans advanced deep learning techniques, including Diffusion Models, Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), LLM quantization, and 3D reconstruction such as 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS).
