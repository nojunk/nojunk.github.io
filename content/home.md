# Machine Intelligence & Pattern Analysis Laboratory

MIPA Laboratory studies machine intelligence and pattern analysis at Seoul National University. The lab homepage is structured as a small Markdown-driven site so notices, member lists, publications, and gallery notes can be edited without touching layout code.

## Research Areas

- **Machine intelligence** - learning algorithms, representations, and intelligent systems.
- **Pattern analysis** - computer vision, recognition, and structured interpretation of data.
- **Multimodal learning** - models that connect images, language, and other signals.
- **Reliable experimentation** - reproducible research practices, clear benchmarks, and careful evaluation.

## Quick Links

- **Notice** - seminar news, recruitment updates, and lab announcements.
- **Members** - professor, students, collaborators, and alumni.
- **Publications** - arXiv, international conferences, journals, domestic publications, theses, patents, and other work.
- **Gallery** - lab events, workshops, celebrations, and research moments.
